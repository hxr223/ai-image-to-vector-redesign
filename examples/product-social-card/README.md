# Product Social Card

Use this case for a GPT Image 2 product promotion card with a useful product render but non-editable layout text.

## Expected Assets

- `original.png`: flattened product card.
- `problem-crop.png`: close crop showing blurry price, product claim, or CTA text.
- `rebuilt-preview.png`: reconstructed social card.
- `detail-comparison.png`: before/after crop comparison.
- `output/`: SVG/PDF/PNG delivery package.

## Reconstruction Notes

- Confirm product name, price, offer terms, CTA, and brand wording.
- Preserve product render as raster unless a clean source asset is available.
- Rebuild title, price, badge, CTA, and frame as editable vector/HTML elements.
- Export square or Xiaohongshu-style PNG presets depending on the target channel.
